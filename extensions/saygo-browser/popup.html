Saygo Browser

Requires Saygo installed on your computer. The extension does not include an AI model.

Connect to let Saygo operate all website tabs in this browser profile, including new tabs and popups. HTTP, WebSocket and SSE traffic is recorded automatically while connected.

Screenshots, page content and network traffic may contain personal data or login tokens. Your local Agent can receive this data and may send it to its AI provider. Connect only a browser profile you want to share.
